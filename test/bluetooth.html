Bluetooth test

Checks that this computer's browser can talk to the payment terminal over Bluetooth. Nothing is paid and nothing is saved: the page only says hello to the terminal and waits for its answer.

This browser

    Before you start

    1. On the P3, open the POS Link sample app. Tap Be the terminal if it says "Cashier", then choose Bluetooth.
    2. Pair the P3 with this computer once, in the computer's Bluetooth settings. On the P3, open Settings → Bluetooth so it's visible while you pair.
    3. Click Connect to the P3 and pick the P3 in the list.
    Not connected

    Follow the steps above.
